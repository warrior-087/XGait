# Gait Video Data Availability

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The video recordings are not currently publicly available due to participant privacy concerns and their substantial size (over 300 GB). We are exploring feasible release options that address privacy protection and data distribution. No release date or access arrangement has been confirmed.

If you need access to the video data for research, please contact us using the contact information in the main [README](../../README.md) to discuss your requirements and potential access options. Contacting us does not guarantee access; any sharing will depend on applicable consent and privacy requirements.
